## 🌟 Our Mission

NASA’s Earth Information Center (EIC) produces breathtaking visualizations of our dynamic planet. However, presenting science solely through visual frames limits who can reach it. Traditional visualizations leave out visually impaired individuals, auditory learners, and audiences seeking deeper multi-sensory connections with climate science.

At **Jutebox**, our mission is to build inclusive, sensory-rich bridges between complex planetary observation and everyday human experience. By pairing real-time scientific telemetry from NASA EIC satellite frames (forest canopy heights, ocean currents, atmospheric aerosols, and global biosphere dynamics) with dynamic Web Audio sonification, we make Earth science accessible, immersive, and unforgettable.

## 🚀 Flagship Project: `soundoftime`

> **Sound of Time — The Retro Earth Archive Jukebox**  
> *A little piece of Earth, far away from home.*

An interactive pixel-art narrative and real-time sonification console. Players experience the story of Eli and his alien companion Koru on the distant planet Erid, restoring a retro jukebox to synthesize authentic NASA Earth science observations into dynamic soundscapes.

```
       ┌────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
       │   RETRO EARTH ARCHIVE JUKEBOX                          │
       │   [003 FOREST]  [002 OCEAN]  [001 ATMOSPHERE]  [004]   │
       │   ▶ PLAY   ⏸ PAUSE   ⏹ STOP   ⏏ EJECT   🔊 [===80%=]   │
       │   💭 ZOOM INTO MEMORY       🔬 HOW WAS THIS MADE?       │
       └────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘
```

### ✨ Key Capabilities
* **Dynamic Procedural Sonification Engine**: Built on Tone.js and the native Web Audio API to translate satellite telemetry (canopy height, vegetation index, wave frequency, wind speed) into harmonious acoustic layers.
* **4 Curated Earth Records**:
  * `001 ATMOSPHERE`: GEOS-5 atmospheric aerosol & weather dynamic sonification.
  * `002 OCEAN`: ECCO perpetual ocean circulation & tidal resonance.
  * `003 FOREST`: GEDI L4B/L3 canopy density, MODIS vegetation index, and bird choruses.
  * `004 EARTH`: Full-planet interconnected planetary harmony.
* **AI Visual Sonification Scanner**: Scan NASA imagery with an interactive multi-spectral pixel-matrix laser scanner that computes luminance, spectral flux, and edge variance into dynamic procedural synthesis.
* **Slow-Mo Memory Cinema**: Interactive audiovisual memory bubbles featuring 4K nature video ambiances and HD canvas simulations.
* **Dual-Mode Experience**: Play the full emotional narrative in Story Mode or launch directly into the Earth Jukebox for scientific exploration.

| :--- | :--- |
